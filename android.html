Vault for Android privacy policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This policy covers Vault for Android, the notes app called "Vault" on Google Play. (Vault for iPhone, iPad and Mac has its own policy, with the same commitments.)

Who we are

Vault is made by EdgeHealth SL ("we", "us"). If you have a question about this policy or about your privacy in Vault, write to us at javier@edgeh.com.

What Vault is

Vault is the app for one person's own Vault server. That server keeps its owner's notes as Markdown files in a folder on the owner's own computer, and Vault lets the owner read, search, edit and create them. The server's address is built into the app, and only its owner can sign in to it. Vault cannot be pointed at another server, no server software is offered, and Vault is not an online service. Anyone else can use only the built-in demo (see "The demo").

What goes where

Your notes, your files (including photos and videos you add to a note) and your sign-in details (what you type to sign in) go only to the Vault server whose address is built into the app. They travel over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Only that server's owner can sign in to it, so the only notes it ever receives are the owner's own.

We receive none of it. Vault has no analytics, telemetry, advertising or tracking. It contains no third-party code that collects data, and it sends nothing to any AI service. We have no copy of your notes, no record of your sign-in, and no way to see what you do in the app. Vault asks Android for one permission only: access to the internet, to reach that server.

What stays on your device

  • Your sign-in session: a token from your server, kept in the app's secure storage, protected by the Android Keystore, and usable on this device only.
  • Your display settings, such as sort orders and card styles. They never contain the names of your notes or folders.
  • A file you hand to the system. A file you open is copied into the app's temporary folder and handed to the app you choose to show it. Download opens Android's "Save to…" screen and saves a copy where you choose; that copy is yours to keep or delete. The temporary copy stays until you open or save the next file, sign out, or start the app again.
  • Unsaved edits: while a note has edits that your Vault server does not have yet, the app keeps them on the device at once, so that no change is lost if the app or the device stops: the note's name and its text, both as your server last sent it and as you edited it (and, when your edits clash with a change made elsewhere, that other version too). They are encrypted with a key that is kept only on this device, in the app's secure storage, and is never moved to another device. They are deleted when your server confirms it has saved the note, when you discard the edits, and when you sign out. The app's data is not included in device backups.
  • Open tabs: so that they come back when the app starts again, the app keeps on the device the names of the notes open in tabs. They are encrypted and stored in the same way as unsaved edits, and deleted when you sign out.
  • Notes in memory: while the app is open, it keeps the notes you open in memory so that they show quickly. They are gone when the app closes. Apart from unsaved edits, Vault does not store your notes on the device.

The demo

Vault has a built-in demo, so that you can try it without a server. Its notes are made up by us and generated on your device when the demo starts. Changes you make in the demo are kept in the app's memory only, until you leave the demo or close the app. Nothing from the demo is sent anywhere, and it never touches your sign-in.

Crash reports

If Vault crashes and you have chosen, in your device's settings, to share usage and diagnostics data with Google, Google Play may give us a crash report. It describes where the app stopped and on which device and system version. It contains no notes. If you have not chosen to share, we receive nothing.

How long data is kept, and deleting it

  • Unsaved edits stay on the device until your server has them saved, until you discard them, or until you sign out.
  • Signing out removes your sign-in session and every unsaved edit from the device. If there are unsaved edits, Vault says so before you sign out.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all it kept on the device, the sign-in session and unsaved edits included.
  • Your notes on your server are yours: you keep or delete them there.
  • We keep nothing about you, so there is nothing for us to delete. If you want to be sure, write to us at the address above.

Children

Vault is not directed at children. We collect no data from anyone, children included.
